Leo Clips Cars is a clipping agency for car YouTubers. We take a creator's long-form videos and cut them into short vertical clips: captioned, credited, and published to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and X.

Many car channels put out excellent long-form content and have little or no short-form presence. That is the gap we work in: the content already exists, it just is not reaching the short-form audience. Every clip we publish exists to send viewers back to the creator's full video and channel.

What we do

  • Find the moments. We study a channel's whole catalogue rather than one video at a time, and select the moments that stand on their own: builds, saves, fails, hard-won advice.
  • Cut for each platform. Every clip gets burned-in captions, the creator's branding on an end card, and platform-specific copy: a TikTok caption is written for TikTok, a Shorts title for YouTube search.
  • Send viewers home. Each clip credits its source and links back to the original video. The clip is the trailer; the creator's channel is the destination.

Source material and permission

Clips are made from long-form videos published by their original creators, used with the creator's written permission, obtained before any clip from their channel is published. Each clip credits its source and links back to the original video. The clips exist to send viewers to that original video, not to replace it.

Who is behind this

Leo Clips Cars is run by one person, in-house, on their own equipment. There is no outsourced clip farm and no volume play: a small number of channels, worked deeply. The publishing software is our own. How it operates, and what it can and cannot do, is described on the how it works page.

Working with us

If you run a car channel and want your back catalogue working for you on short-form, see the for creators page for how an engagement works, or get in touch directly. Common questions are answered in the FAQ.
