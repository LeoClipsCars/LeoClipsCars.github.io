AIOS Clip Poster

Short-form video publishing tool

AIOS Clip Poster is a small, self-hosted tool that publishes short-form video clips to the social media accounts its operator owns.

It is run by one person on their own computer. It is not a product, not a hosted service, and it has no sign-up — there are no users other than the operator.

What it does

Long-form videos are cut into short vertical clips with captions. This tool takes those finished clips and uploads them, on a schedule the operator controls, to accounts the operator runs on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

  • Every upload is started deliberately by the operator. Nothing runs unattended.
  • It posts only to accounts the operator owns and has personally connected.
  • It reads nothing from other people's accounts and collects no data about them.

Source material and permission

Clips are made from long-form videos published by their original creators, used with the creator's written permission, obtained before any clip from their channel is published. Each clip credits its source and links back to the original video. The tool exists to send viewers to that original video, not to replace it.

How platform accounts are connected

Connections are made through each platform's own official sign-in flow. The operator signs in on the platform's website and approves access there. The tool receives an access token, which is stored on the operator's own computer and used only to upload clips to that account.

No account passwords are stored by this tool, and none are needed.
