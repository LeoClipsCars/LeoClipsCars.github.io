Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

AIOS Clip Poster is a self-hosted tool run by a single individual on their own computer. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no visitors other than its operator. This policy describes the small amount of data it handles.

This website collects nothing. It is a set of static pages. There are no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no embedded third-party scripts, and no forms.

What the tool stores

When the operator connects one of their own social media accounts, the platform issues an access token. That token is stored in a file on the operator's computer and is used only to upload the operator's own video clips to that same account.

  • Tokens are stored locally. They are not transmitted anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.
  • No account passwords are stored or requested at any point.
  • Tokens can be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data belonging to any other person.
  • It does not read other users' accounts, followers, messages or activity.
  • It does not build profiles, run advertising, or perform analytics on individuals.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone. There is no data to share.

Video content

The tool uploads video files that the operator has prepared on their own computer. Those files are sent to the platform the operator is publishing to, and to nowhere else. Publicly available information returned by a platform about the operator's own post — such as its identifier and status — is kept locally so the same clip is not posted twice.

Third parties

The only third parties involved are the social media platforms the operator publishes to. Once a clip is published, that platform's own privacy policy governs it.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its operator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to data, can be sent to leoclipscars@gmail.com.
