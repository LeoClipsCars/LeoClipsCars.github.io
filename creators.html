For creators

Your footage, your channel, your call. Permission in writing before anything posts.

Permission comes first

Nothing from a channel is published without its creator's agreement. Clips are made from long-form videos published by their original creators, used with the creator's written permission, obtained before any clip from their channel is published. A message from you saying yes is enough to count as written. The point is that there is a record both sides can point to.

Every clip works for your channel

  • Every clip credits you as the source and links back to the original video.
  • Captions never spoil the full video. A clip that gives away the ending has no reason to send anyone to your channel.
  • Your voice is your voice: clips are your material, cut faithfully. Captions we write claim nothing your footage does not support.
  • You can see how your clips perform. What gets cut, where it goes, and what it does there is never a black box.

Your boundaries are recorded and respected

If there are subjects you do not want clipped, moments you want left alone, or a tone you want kept, that is recorded when we start and it is applied to every clip after. Where a moment raises a question your standing answer does not cover, we ask you rather than guess.

Removal on request

You can change your mind. If a creator asks for material to be removed, it is removed. Write to leoclipscars@gmail.com with a link to the clip.

How an engagement works

Terms are agreed directly with each creator. There is no sign-up, no self-serve dashboard, and no standard package forced onto every channel. If you run a car channel with a strong back catalogue and little short-form presence, get in touch with a link to your channel and we will take a look before we talk.
